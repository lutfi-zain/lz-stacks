# Twitter/X Playbook

A research-backed reference for writing high-performing posts and threads on Twitter/X.

## 1. Algorithm Insights (2025/2026)
- **Bookmark Weighting:** Bookmarking is the strongest positive signal in the recommendation engine. Content must be reference-grade (cheatsheets, resource lists, step-by-step guides) to earn bookmarks.
- **Replies vs. Likes:** The algorithm prioritizes active conversations. A post with 10 replies and 5 likes will outperform a post with 50 likes and 0 replies.
- **Thread Penalty:** The algorithm occasionally reduces the reach of long threads containing external links in early tweets. Always place links in the final tweet of the thread.
- **Dwell Time:** The system measures seconds spent on a post. Threads naturally score high on this metric.

---

## 2. Platform Formats
- **Single Post (280 characters):** Best for contrarian takes, punchy observations, and quick code snippets.
- **Threads:** The primary format for high-context value delivery. Recommend 5-9 tweets per thread.
- **Polls:** High conversion rates for engagement, best used to decide the next content topic.

---

## 3. Thread Architecture
Every successful thread must follow the **Hook → Value → CTA** structure:

### Tweet 1: The Hook (280 Chars Max)
Must stand completely alone as a compelling statement. Use **The Bridge Method** to front-load the result or tension. Do not include links or hashtags in Tweet 1.
- *Example:* "I built a trading bot that ran for 90 days. It made 142 trades and closed at +18.4%. Here is the exact mathematical model and the Python codebase I used (all open source):"

### Tweet 2 to N-1: The Value Beats
Deliver the system, code, or framework step-by-step. Keep each tweet focused on ONE clear idea. Use code blocks or screenshots for visual relief.

### Tweet N: The CTA
The final tweet is where you call the reader to action:
- Ask for a Retweet on Tweet 1 to expand reach.
- Provide the link to your newsletter, repository, or product.

---

## 4. Platform Specifications
- **Character Limit:** 280 characters (standard) or 25,000 characters (Premium). Stick to 280-character chunks even when drafting premium posts to ensure readability.
- **Images:** 1600x900 pixels (16:9 ratio) is the optimal display dimension for inline media.
- **Hashtags:** Avoid hashtags entirely. They look like ads and lower click-through rates.
