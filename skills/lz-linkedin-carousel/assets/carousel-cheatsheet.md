# LinkedIn Carousel Pre-Flight Checklist

Use this cheatsheet to review a carousel draft before export or upload.

---

## 1. Copywriting Checklist

- [ ] **Billboard Hook Check:** Is the hook focused on a single outcome/stat rather than a generic process? (e.g. *"Why 90% of X fail"* vs *"How to do X"*)
- [ ] **Slide Count Check:** Is the slide count between 6 and 10 slides?
- [ ] **Dwell-Time Flow:** Does each slide transition invite the next swipe (e.g. trailing question, "But here is the catch...")?
- [ ] **Text Density:** Is there less than 35 words per slide? Are complex sentences broken down?
- [ ] **No AI Slop:** Have words like *tapestry, delve, leverage, paradigm, showcase, seamless* been removed?
- [ ] **Caption Bridge:** Does the caption bridge the reader from the feed directly into the carousel?
- [ ] **Recap Checklist:** Is slide 9 a bento-style checklist/summary box?
- [ ] **Single CTA:** Does the final slide have exactly *one* call-to-action?

---

## 2. Visual Design Checklist

- [ ] **Safe Zone Verification:** Are all text blocks and images at least 80px away from the 1080x1350px boundaries?
- [ ] **Typography Scale:** Are titles distinct and at least 32px? Is body text highly readable?
- [ ] **The 3-Color Rule:**
  - Background (60%): Slate/Deep Navy (`#0B0F19`) or warm cream/off-white (`#FAF9F6`).
  - Structural lines & primary text (30%).
  - Tech Accent (10%): Single vibrant accent.
- [ ] **Bento Radii:** Are cards rounded (`16px` to `24px` border-radius)?
- [ ] **Border Stroke:** Are borders thin (`1px solid` or low opacity)?
- [ ] **UI Graphic Desaturation:** Are screenshot mockups desaturated/monochromatic, with only key highlights in the accent color?
- [ ] **Chevron & Arrows:** Does each intermediate slide have a right-edge arrow pointing to the next slide?
- [ ] **Page Numbers:** Are page numbers (`X of Y`) visible on the top or bottom right corner?
- [ ] **Brand Handle:** Is the personal/company handle (`@handle`) visible on the bottom left corner?
