# YouTube Ideation System

A structured process to generate, filter, and prioritize long-form video concepts optimized for YouTube's discovery algorithms.

## 1. Search Plays vs. Browse Plays

### Search Plays (Evergreen Authority)
- **Target:** Direct query searches (e.g., *"how to setup cloudflare email routing"*).
- **Goal:** Drive consistent, evergreen traffic that accumulates views over months or years.
- **Metric focus:** SEO ranking, clear keyword targeting, high-value tutorial frameworks.

### Browse Plays (Suggested & Home Feed)
- **Target:** Browse feeds, suggested reels, and home page.
- **Goal:** Drive high-velocity view spikes by leveraging curiosity, industry trends, or contrarian opinions.
- **Metric focus:** CTR (Click-Through Rate), first-hour velocity, emotional hook resonance.

---

## 2. Generating the 10-Idea List
When brainstorming new video concepts, each entry must explicitly document the discovery mechanism:

1. **Working Title Direction:** A descriptive concept angle (not the final title).
2. **Intent:** Search, Browse, or Hybrid.
3. **Discovery Logic:** The specific search query or browse interest this video targets.
4. **The Promise:** The concrete value or outcome the viewer gets by watching.
5. **Content Pillar:** Alignment with your primary niche pillars.
6. **Format Type:** Tutorial, breakdown, personal story, or case study.
7. **Hook Direction:** A climax-led cold open angle.

---

## 3. Prioritizing the Queue
- Filter out ideas that do not align with your current strategic goals (from STRATEGY.md).
- Avoid repeating topics covered in your last 5 videos unless you have a fresh, contrarian angle.
- Identify the single **"Make This Next"** candidate: the video with the highest estimated search volume or topical relevance today.
