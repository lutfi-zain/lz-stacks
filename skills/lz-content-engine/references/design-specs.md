# Design Specifications

A visual design reference for content creators, detailing platform dimensions, layout guidelines, typography principles, and color psychology.

## 1. Platform Dimension Cheat Sheet

| Platform | Format | Dimensions | Ratio | Safe Zone Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Document (PDF) | 1080x1350 px | 4:5 | Keep text 80px away from edges |
| **LinkedIn** | Post Image | 1200x627 px | 1.91:1 | Landscape preview size |
| **Twitter/X** | Single Image | 1600x900 px | 16:9 | Inline display ratio |
| **Instagram** | Carousel Slide | 1080x1350 px | 4:5 | Preferred over 1:1 for screen real estate |
| **Instagram** | Reel/Story | 1080x1920 px | 9:16 | Avoid top 200px and bottom 300px (UI overlays) |

## 2. Typography Rules
- **Legibility is King:** Body copy on mobile screens is read quickly. Use highly legible sans-serif fonts (e.g., Inter, Roboto, Helvetica). Never use display, handwritten, or serif fonts for body text blocks.
- **The Golden Ratio of Scale:** Make display headlines 2.5x to 3.x larger than body copy to establish clear visual hierarchy.
- **Line Length:** Keep body copy lines between 45-60 characters. Lines that are too long cause reading fatigue; lines that are too short break flow.

---

## 3. Visual Hierarchy & Composition
- **F-Pattern Reading:** Users scan text in an "F" shape (top left, across, then down the left). Place your hook and focal branding in these zones.
- **Whitespace / Negative Space:** Leave at least 30% of the canvas empty. Whitespace directs the eye to the focal point and makes the design feel premium.
- **Contrast Check:** Ensure text-to-background contrast satisfies WCAG AA requirements (minimum 4.5:1 ratio for body copy).

---

## 4. Color Psychology for Social Branding
- **Dominant Color (60%):** The background tone. Typically dark slate, cream, or off-white. Sets the overall mood.
- **Structure Color (30%):** Used for borders, card backdrops, and standard text. Builds readability.
- **Accent Color (10%):** High-saturation tone (e.g., neon yellow, vibrant orange, electric blue). Used strictly to highlight headings, keywords, or the final CTA button.
- **Consistency Rule:** Never change the brand colors between slides. Slide 1 and Slide 10 must belong to the same visual universe.
