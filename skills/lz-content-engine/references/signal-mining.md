# Signal Mining

A systematic framework to filter raw inputs (news, tweets, Reddit threads, scraping digests) and extract high-value content angles tailored to your niche.

## Signal vs. Noise
- **Noise:** Generic reporting or repeating what happened. E.g., *"OpenAI released a new model."*
- **Signal:** The unique opinion, contrarian frame, or practical takeaway that directly affects your audience. E.g., *"The new OpenAI model kills the 'AI can't write code' excuse — here is what that means for software developers who still refuse to use copilots."*

---

## The Mining Process

### 1. Niche Filtering
Evaluate raw inputs strictly against your content pillars. Ignore trending news that does not intersect with your primary themes, even if the engagement numbers are high.

### 2. Extracting the Take
For every signal you identify, do not just summarize. Define your **take**:
- What is the common reaction to this news?
- What are they getting wrong?
- What is the hidden opportunity or danger for your target reader?

### 3. Formatting and Hooking
- Assign each signal to a platform-native format (e.g., a tweet thread for high-context news, a Reel for a quick hot take).
- Draft a climax-led hook immediately using **The Bridge Method**.

---

## Output structure for Mining Reports
When processing a feed dump, document findings as follows:
- **Niche/Pillars Checked:** [Pillar list]
- **The Signals Worth Posting About:**
  1. **Angle:** [The specific take]
  2. **The Raw Signal:** [Link or quote from input]
  3. **Why It Matters:** [Relevance to target audience]
  4. **Format + Hook:** [e.g., LinkedIn Carousel + Bridge Hook]
- **Noise to Ignore:** [List of trending topics in the niche you are skipping and why]
- **The Fastest Win:** [The time-sensitive post to create and publish today]
