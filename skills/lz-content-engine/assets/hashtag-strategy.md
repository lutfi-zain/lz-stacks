# Hashtag Strategy per Platform

A reference template for hashtag usage, volume tiering, and pillar mapping.

---

## 1. Platform Hashtag Rules

| Platform | Recommended Qty | Placement | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | 3-5 max | Very bottom of post | Mix broad and specific. Do not use inline hashtags in sentences. |
| **Twitter/X** | 0-1 max | End of post (if any) | Avoid entirely. Hashtags degrade click-through rates. |
| **Instagram** | 3-5 max | Bottom of caption | Place in caption, not comment section. Keep them highly targeted. |

---

## 2. Hashtag Volume Tiers

- **Tier 1 (Broad/Mega - 1M+ posts):** e.g., `#developer`, `#marketing`. Sets broad category context. Use max 1.
- **Tier 2 (Niche/Medium - 100k-1M posts):** e.g., `#cloudflareworkers`, `#systemdesign`. Targets active interest groups. Use 2.
- **Tier 3 (Specific/Micro - <100k posts):** e.g., `#agenticworkflows`, `#manychathacks`. Highly targeted conversions. Use 1-2.
- **Tier 4 (Brand-Specific):** e.g., `#lzcontentengine`. Strictly for your own brand.
