# LinkedIn Playbook

A research-backed reference for writing and formatting high-converting content on LinkedIn.

## 1. Algorithm Insights (2025/2026)
- **Dwell Time:** The algorithm tracks how long a user spends reading your post. Long-form posts, structured lists, and document carousels (PDFs) naturally capture high dwell time.
- **Dwell vs. Click:** "Show more" clicks are heavily weighted signals. Your first 3 lines (the hook before the fold) must force this click.
- **Engagement Velocity:** Likes, comments, and shares within the first 60 minutes of posting determine the post's reach. Comments are weighted significantly higher than likes.
- **Link Penalty:** External links in the post body are occasionally penalized. The recommended practice is placing high-value links in the body only when contextually critical, or using the "link in comments" method while pin-pointing it.

---

## 2. Platform Formats
- **Text Posts:** Clean formatting, generous line breaks, and bullet-point summaries. Best for short insights.
- **Document Carousels (PDFs):** Multi-page PDF slide decks (1080x1080 or 1080x1350). These drive the highest dwell time and sharing rates.
- **Newsletter/Articles:** Best for deeply technical deep-dives (>1,500 words).
- **Polls:** Best for quick audience research and engagement velocity.

---

## 3. Formatting Specifications
- **Character Limit:** 3,000 characters per post.
- **The Fold:** The first 140-210 characters (typically 3 lines) appear before the "... see more" button. Place your strongest climax-led hook here.
- **Carousels:** Use 5-10 slides max. Clean margins, one central focus per slide.
- **Hashtags:** Use 3-5 hashtags max. Place them at the very bottom. Do not mix them into the copy.

---

## 4. Copywriting Rules
- **Formatting:** Break text every 1-2 sentences. Avoid blocky paragraphs.
- **The Visual Hook:** Use symbols or whitespace to draw the eye down the post.
- **CTAs:** Route to a single CTA (e.g., "Get the template link in the comments" or "Comment 'AUDIT' and I'll send it to you").
- **Language:** Maintain a professional, competent, yet direct tone. Eliminate corporate buzzwords ("synergy", "paradigm shift", "leverage").
