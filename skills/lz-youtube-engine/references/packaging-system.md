# YouTube Packaging System

A methodology to generate and critique high-CTR YouTube titles and thumbnail-word pairs.

## The Pairing Principle
A viewer sees the thumbnail and the title together. If they both communicate the same message, you have wasted visual real estate. They must perform distinct, complementary roles:

- **Title (Context & SEO):** Communicates the keywords and the algorithm-readable value. Length must remain under 60 characters to prevent truncation on mobile.
- **Thumbnail Words (Curiosity & Human Emotion):** 1-3 high-impact words. Creates cognitive dissonance, curiosity, or emotional resonance.
- **Rule:** The title and thumbnail words must NEVER share the same words.

---

## Generate Mode
When packaging a video topic, construct 3 distinct pairs across these angles:

1. **Curiosity-led:** Focus on a mystery or high-contrast statement.
2. **Benefit-led:** Focus on a specific metric or outcome.
3. **Identity/Contrarian-led:** Focus on a belief shift or industry critique.

### Output Structure
For each pair, document:
- **Title:** [text]
- **Thumbnail Words:** [1-3 words]
- **The Pairing Logic:** [Explanation of how the title and thumbnail do different jobs and combine to drive clicks]

---

## Critique Mode
When evaluating existing or draft packaging assets:
- **Pass/Fix/Fail Verdict:** Assess readability and keyword strength.
- **Pairing Check:** Are the thumbnail words duplicating the title?
- **Character Count:** Is the title under 60 characters?
- **Provide 3 revised fixes:** Specific pairing adjustments to optimize CTR.
