# Series Planning

How to turn a broad content topic into a cohesive multi-part series mapped across platforms. This prevents you from burning a major theme in a single post and builds a binge loop that keeps followers on your profile.

## The Series Mix
Each series in your content calendar must serve a specific job to move the user down the funnel:

1. **Credibility Series (Authority/Depth):** Prove you know what you are talking about. Deep-dives, breakdowns, case studies, and code architectures. Slow-burn, high trust.
2. **Shareability Series (Virality/Reach):** Built to be saved, shared, and DM'd. Checklists, tools, references, cheatsheets, and industry summaries.
3. **Conversion Series (Action/Leads):** Direct pitch posts built to get the user to sign up, comment a ManyChat keyword, click a link, or buy.

---

## Designing the Series Arc

### 1. Define the pay-off
Every series must build toward a final realization, an offer launch, or a major CTA. Do not start planning without a clear Day 30 destination.

### 2. Map the 4-6 Parts
Break the topic down into 4 to 6 connected steps or parts.
- **Part 1:** Introduce the tension and the core hook.
- **Part 2-4:** Intermediate steps, alternating credibility and shareability.
- **Part 5:** The conversion pitch, leveraging the context built in parts 1-4.

### 3. Create the Binge Loop
Explicitly link the parts. At the end of Part 2, refer back to Part 1 and tease Part 3. In carousels, display a mini index of the series. This ensures a reader who discovers Part 4 immediately seeks out Parts 1-3.

---

## Platform Mapping Guide
- **YouTube:** Deep context, execution walkthroughs, and screen-shares.
- **Instagram:** Visual carousels showing step-by-step systems; short Reels for hooks.
- **LinkedIn:** Text breakdowns with clean layout, document PDFs (slides).
- **Newsletter:** High-value commentary, personal stories, and the direct conversion offer.
