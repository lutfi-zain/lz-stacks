# Story Mining

A structured process to extract multiple content angles from a single personal experience, client result, or raw event. Lived experience is your most original content source because it cannot be replicated by AI.

## The 5 Story Angles
A single raw story usually contains up to five distinct content angles. Do not limit yourself to a basic chronological retelling ("here is what happened").

### 1. The Lesson (Direct Education)
Teach the main takeaway directly to the audience. This is the most obvious angle.
- *Focus:* "I did X, and learned Y. Here is the lesson."

### 2. The Contrarian Take (Industry Critique)
Use the story to challenge conventional wisdom or a popular belief in your niche.
- *Focus:* "Everyone tells you to do X. But when I did X, this went wrong. Here is why that advice is broken."

### 3. The Framework (The System)
Extract the repeatable steps or methodology from the experience so the reader can replicate the success.
- *Focus:* "I solved this problem using a 3-step framework. Here is how you can use it."

### 4. The Proof / Case Study (Authority Building)
Frame the story as concrete evidence for a claim or service you offer.
- *Focus:* "Here is the exact data and timeline of how we achieved this result."

### 5. The Relatable Moment (Human Connection)
Focus on the raw emotion, vulnerability, or failure in the story to build an emotional bond.
- *Focus:* "The moment I wanted to quit, what I was thinking, and the realization that changed my direction."

---

## Mining Process
1. **Log the Raw Story:** Write down the experience in a messy, unstructured format.
2. **Scan for Angles:** Identify which of the five angles are genuinely supported by the facts (do not fabricate details).
3. **Draft the Climax Hook:** Use **The Bridge Method** to write the opening for each angle.
4. **Determine the Lead Post:** Select the strongest angle to publish first.
5. **Protect Rare Assets:** If the story contains a highly unique event or lesson, do not waste it in a brief, low-context post. Save it for a long-form article, newsletter, or video outline.
