---

## Platform Adaptations

| Target Platform | Native Format | Angle & Framing | Climax-led Hook Draft | CTA Mechanism | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Document Carousel | Professional/data-led breakdown of the system. Focus on metrics and authority. | [Hook] | Direct link to primary source | High |
| **Twitter/X** | Thread (5-7 tweets) | Contrarian take on the topic. Focus on punchy steps and bookmark-worthy metrics. | [Hook] | RT Tweet 1 + opt-in link | High |
| **Instagram** | Visual Carousel | Step-by-step visual slide deck. Focus on visual structure. | [Hook] | ManyChat comment keyword | Medium |
| **Instagram** | Reel (15-30s) | Pattern interrupt video explaining the top mistake of this concept. | [Hook] | Comment keyword + caption CTA | Low |

---

## Repurposing Workflow
1. Write the **Primary Source** first to establish deep context.
2. Identify the core signal.
3. Map the target platforms and write custom hooks (do not copy-paste the same hook across platforms).
4. Adapt the body copy to match each platform's length constraints and visual style.
